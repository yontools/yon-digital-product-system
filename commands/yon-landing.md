# /yon-landing

## Purpose
Create or improve a landing page focused on understanding, trust and conversion.

## Audit

Review:
- value proposition clarity;
- audience relevance;
- hierarchy and scanning;
- proof and credibility;
- objections and risk reduction;
- CTA clarity;
- mobile experience;
- visual identity;
- content density;
- performance;
- accessibility.

## Rule

Every major section should have a job. Remove sections that do not improve understanding, trust or the desired action.

Use real, relevant imagery when it communicates the product better than generic graphics.

Verify the final page on desktop and mobile.
