# /yon-motion

## Purpose
Introduce motion only where it improves understanding, feedback, continuity or perceived quality.

## Review

Consider:
- entrance and transition timing;
- state changes;
- loading feedback;
- navigation continuity;
- micro-interactions;
- scroll storytelling;
- reduced-motion preferences;
- performance on low-powered devices.

## Rules

Motion is subordinate to usability.

Do not animate every element. Prefer a small number of coherent motion principles over unrelated effects.

Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid animations that block critical actions.

Verify motion in the real browser and on relevant device sizes.
