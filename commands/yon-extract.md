---
description: Extract generalized reusable product knowledge from evidence into a reviewable YON pattern or capability candidate without exposing private implementation details.
---

Use the `yon-extract` skill.

Process the relevant project evidence and produce an extraction candidate. Inspect existing YON patterns and capabilities first. Distinguish observation, inference, hypothesis, and outcome. Generalize private implementation details and run the privacy check.

Do not publish or promote the result automatically. The output must clearly state whether the candidate is a pattern, capability, product-specific insight, or needs more evidence, plus confidence, limitations, evidence status, and proposed destination.
