# /yon-saas

## Purpose
Design, build or audit a SaaS as an operational system rather than a collection of screens.

## Inspect

- tenant/company model;
- users, roles and permissions;
- onboarding;
- navigation and information architecture;
- dashboard and operational views;
- CRUD workflows;
- search, filters and sorting;
- tables and forms;
- workflow/state transitions;
- notifications;
- activity and history;
- settings;
- billing when applicable;
- security;
- responsive behavior.

## Optimization

Look for repeated tasks that can be improved through automation, sensible defaults, bulk actions, shortcuts, contextual actions and useful system-generated information.

## Principle

The interface should reduce the operational burden of the people doing the work.

Use the YON operating loop and verify critical workflows in the running product.
