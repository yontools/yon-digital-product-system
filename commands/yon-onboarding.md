# /yon-onboarding

## Purpose
Design onboarding that gets users to meaningful value quickly without creating unnecessary friction.

## Instructions

Identify the user's first meaningful outcome and design the shortest safe path to it.

Review:
- initial goal;
- minimum information required;
- sensible defaults;
- progressive disclosure;
- import/setup shortcuts;
- first useful result;
- contextual help;
- recovery from mistakes;
- ability to continue later.

Prefer onboarding embedded in the first real task over a long tutorial when possible.

Do not force onboarding steps that do not contribute to activation, safety or required configuration.
