# Contributing to YON

YON is designed to improve through reviewed, reusable knowledge.

## What belongs here

- general product principles;
- reusable UX/UI patterns;
- implementation guidance that is not tied to a private project;
- experiments and findings that can be generalized;
- QA practices;
- templates and examples that do not contain confidential information.

## What does not belong here

- API keys or credentials;
- private customer information;
- proprietary source code from client projects;
- private business rules;
- internal URLs or infrastructure secrets;
- copied material that cannot legally be redistributed.

## Pattern quality

New patterns should explain the problem, context, solution, trade-offs, limitations and verification method. Mark uncertain ideas as `experimental` rather than presenting them as established practice.

## Review philosophy

Prefer evidence and clear reasoning over novelty. A smaller library of useful patterns is better than a large library of untested suggestions.
