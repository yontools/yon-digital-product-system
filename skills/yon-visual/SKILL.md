# YON Visual

Use visual assets when they improve understanding, trust, identity, or emotional impact. Prefer bespoke and context-relevant imagery over generic stock or decorative visuals. Review composition, hierarchy, typography, contrast, consistency, responsive cropping, loading behavior, and accessibility. Keep visuals subordinate to the product goal.
