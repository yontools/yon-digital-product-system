# YON SaaS

Treat the SaaS as an operating system for a business process, not a collection of screens. Understand roles and jobs, map the critical workflows, reduce repeated work, identify automation and bulk actions, and design clear states and permissions. Reuse proven YON capabilities and patterns. Implement incrementally and verify real workflows in the running product.
