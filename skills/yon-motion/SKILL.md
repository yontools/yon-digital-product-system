# YON Motion

Use motion to communicate state, continuity, hierarchy, feedback, or delight—not as decoration by default. Prefer small, coherent moments over scattered effects. Respect reduced-motion preferences, avoid blocking important interactions, and verify performance across responsive states. Choose the existing project's motion tooling when appropriate rather than adding dependencies without need.
