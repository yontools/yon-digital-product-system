# YON Landing

Understand the offer, audience, objection, desired action, and proof available. Build a clear message hierarchy and a focused conversion path. Use visuals that communicate the product rather than generic decoration. Make trust, pricing or next steps clear when appropriate. Verify the page at relevant viewport sizes and inspect accessibility, loading, interaction, and conversion-critical states.
